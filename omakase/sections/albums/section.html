The album grid, and omamp behind it

- STOCK open a browser, find the album, hope the tab survives the day
  • +a curated grid of albums, fed by my dashboard, on one key
  • +on the NAS → MPD plays it; anywhere else → Apple Music opens to it
  • +album links on the dashboard itself open on this machine, not in a tab

A personal dashboard already knew what I was listening to: featured slots, favourites, new releases from KEXP. omamp puts that grid on the desktop as a Quickshell overlay, keyboard first, in the spirit of Winamp and a folder of MP3s.

The dashboard stays the truth for curation. The NAS is the truth for what exists locally. omamp is the match layer between them, and the split is what keeps either side from having to know about the other.

what it is
Quickshell overlay + MPD + a sync that pulls curation from the dashboard's API.
the trick
Dashboard album links hit a local listener over Tailscale Funnel, so a click on a web page summons the player here.
fallback
Apple Music as a Helium PWA, summoned into the same slot. The reason Widevine is installed.

THE TOLL

Leaving Chrome has one toll, and it lands on music. Helium doesn't bundle Widevine, Google's DRM module, so Apple Music won't play until you fetch the copy Mozilla distributes for Firefox and point Helium at it with a one-line JSON file. To get away from Google's browser, you borrow Google's DRM from Firefox. The browser above cuts away what I didn't ask for; this is the one piece I had to tape back in.
